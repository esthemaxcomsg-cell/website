How to use Hydrojelly

Mixed fresh for every treatment. A few rules make the difference between a good mask and a great one.

1

Mix

1½ measuring cups of powder to 1 measuring cup of cold distilled water. Never warm water, and no other liquids.

2

Prep

Apply ampoules or serums under the mask to boost absorption. Ask the client whether to cover eyes and lips.

3

Apply

Spread straight away, the mask starts to set within 5 to 6 minutes. Work upward for a lifting effect and go thicker at the edges for easy peeling.

4

Rest

Leave on for at least 15 minutes.

5

Peel

Lift in one piece in the direction of hair growth, never against it. Covering the whole face makes removal easier.

6

Clean up

Never put Hydrojelly in the sink. Wipe excess into the bin with a paper towel, then wipe the bowl with a damp towel.

Mixing ratio
1½cups powder
+
1cup water
