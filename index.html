Start from your skin goal. Use the Hydrate, Brighten, Clarify, Calm and Firm filters in the Hydrojelly collection to narrow the range, then compare each formula’s targeted actives. If you are unsure where to begin, Hyaluronic Acid is the universal choice, deeply replenishing for every skin type.

Every mask is mixed fresh. The powder is whisked with water into a cool, silky jelly, applied generously over the face and lifted away in one satisfying piece after 15–20 minutes, sealing in the serums beneath.

Hydrojelly masks are formulated with refined algae, electrolytes and organic active ingredients, and many formulas, such as Pure Himalayan White Tea, Cica Complexion Care and Wildcrafted Sea Moss, are created specifically for sensitive, easily irritated skin.
