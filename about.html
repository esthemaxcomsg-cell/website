The Hydrojelly Difference

Hydrojelly masks are infused with electrolytes to boost skin hydration, just drinking water is not enough. Each mask forms a vacuum-like seal that compresses facial contours, allowing complete absorption of organic nutrition and electrolytes into the deep layers of the skin.

Formulated with refined algae, electrolytes and organic active ingredients, the masks have an instant cooling and soothing effect that is not only refreshing, but helps firm, soothe and revitalize the skin for a natural, subtle glow.

We prefer to avoid the chemicals and preservatives, like parabens, found in gel and liquid activators. Our dry powder formulas keep every ingredient fresh while retaining all the natural benefits, each mask is simply activated with water, mixed fresh for every single treatment.

Our history

1995
